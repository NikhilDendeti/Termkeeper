VENDOR RETAINER AGREEMENT

This Agreement governs the ongoing retainer engagement between Solace Bookkeeping Services ("Vendor") and the undersigned client ("Client") for monthly bookkeeping and compliance support.

1. SERVICES
Vendor shall provide monthly bookkeeping reconciliation, GST filing support, and financial reporting as described in the attached scope document.

2. RECURRING PAYMENT
Client shall pay Vendor a fixed retainer of INR 18,000 per month, billed monthly in advance via an authorized UPI Autopay mandate. This mandate authorizes a maximum charge of INR 18,000 per billing cycle.

3. TERM
This engagement shall continue for an initial term of twelve (12) months from the mandate authorization date, after which it shall convert to a month-to-month arrangement unless either party gives thirty (30) days' notice.

4. PENALTY FOR EARLY TERMINATION
Should Client terminate this Agreement before completing six (6) months of service, Client shall pay an early termination fee equal to two (2) months' retainer.

5. SCOPE CHANGES
Any request for services outside the defined scope shall be billed separately at Vendor's then-current hourly rate, subject to Client's prior written approval.

6. NOTICE OF NON-PAYMENT
If a scheduled payment fails, Vendor shall notify Client and Client shall have five (5) business days to remedy the failure before Vendor may suspend services.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to keep confidential any financial or business information disclosed in the course of this engagement.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, with the seat of arbitration in Bengaluru.
