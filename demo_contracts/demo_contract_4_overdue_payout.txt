FREELANCE RETAINER AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into between Meridian Publishing House ("Client") and the undersigned freelance writer ("Contractor") for ongoing monthly content writing services.

1. SCOPE OF WORK
Contractor shall deliver up to eight (8) articles per month per the editorial calendar agreed with Client.

2. PAYMENT SCHEDULE
Client shall pay Contractor a fixed retainer of INR 20,000 per month, paid via direct payout on or before the 5th day of each month for the preceding month's work.

3. LATE PAYMENT
If payment is not made within the period in Section 2, a late fee of 2% per week shall accrue on the outstanding balance until paid in full.

4. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement with fifteen (15) days' written notice.

5. AUTO-RENEWAL
This Agreement automatically renews each month unless either party provides written notice at least seven (7) days before the renewal date.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation between the parties within ten (10) business days before either party pursues further action.
