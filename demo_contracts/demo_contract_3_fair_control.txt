INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Verdant Studio ("Client") and the undersigned contractor ("Contractor") for freelance illustration services.

1. SCOPE OF WORK
Contractor shall deliver a set of five illustrations per the brief agreed in writing, in two milestones of equal size.

2. PAYMENT SCHEDULE
Client shall pay Contractor INR 25,000 per milestone within 7 days of Contractor's delivery and Client's approval. Approval shall not be unreasonably withheld and shall be deemed given if Client does not respond within 5 business days.

3. LATE PAYMENT
If payment is not made within the period in Section 2, a late fee of 1.5% per week shall apply, mirroring the notice period each party owes the other under Section 4.

4. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement with fourteen (14) days' written notice. Client shall pay for all work completed and approved up to the termination date.

5. AUTO-RENEWAL
This Agreement does not auto-renew. It concludes upon delivery and payment of the final milestone.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute shall first be discussed in good faith between the parties within ten (10) business days before either party pursues further action.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Upon full payment, all rights to the delivered illustrations transfer to Client. Contractor retains the right to display the work in a portfolio.
