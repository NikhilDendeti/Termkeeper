FREELANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into between Northbridge Creative Studio ("Client") and the undersigned independent contractor ("Contractor") for the provision of UI/UX design and frontend development services.

1. SCOPE OF WORK
Contractor shall design and implement a responsive web application dashboard per the specifications provided in Exhibit A, delivered across three milestones.

2. PAYMENT SCHEDULE
Client shall pay Contractor INR 45,000 per milestone. Payment for each completed and approved milestone shall be released within 7 days of Client's written approval of that milestone's deliverables.

3. LATE PAYMENT
In the event Client fails to remit payment within the period specified in Section 2, a late fee of 2% per week shall accrue on the outstanding balance until paid in full.

4. TERMINATION
Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, with immediate effect and no further payment obligation beyond work already approved. Contractor may terminate this Agreement only upon providing thirty (30) days' written notice to Client.

5. AUTO-RENEWAL
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive three-month terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least fifteen (15) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation between the parties. If unresolved within fifteen (15) days, either party may pursue mediation before any other remedy.

7. INDEMNIFICATION
Contractor agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Client from any claims arising from Contractor's breach of this Agreement.

8. MILESTONE ACCEPTANCE
A milestone shall be deemed complete upon Client's written approval. If Client does not respond within a reasonable time, the milestone shall not be deemed automatically approved.
